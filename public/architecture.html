Sentry Runtime Security Architecture

A policy enforcement layer between AI banking agents and core banking services

User

Customer or bank employee

→

AI Banking Agent

Proposes a structured banking action

Never calls the bank directly

→

Sentry Middleware

Evaluates identity, risk, scope, and trajectory

Stores audit and cross-session history

→

Core Banking System

Receives only approved actions

Mock service in this prototype

1. Authorization Context

Verifies the principal, permissions, account scope, and requested resource. Unauthorized actions are blocked at the action boundary.

2. Reversibility Gate

Classifies actions as READ, WRITE, or DESTRUCTIVE. Critical actions wait for explicit supervisor approval.

3. Behavioral Trajectory

Tracks escalation, denied attempts, scope drift, and principal activity across sessions.

EXECUTE · Safe and authorized
REQUIRE CONFIRMATION · Critical action
BLOCK · Unauthorized or unsafe
Sentry gives AI agents useful capabilities without giving them unchecked authority.
